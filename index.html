Draw Squares. Merge Crates. Clear the Board.

Cargo Square Match is a fun and satisfying puzzle game where you interact with a colorful grid of crates. Your goal is simple yet addictive: draw a square around crates of the same type to match and clear them from the board. Form squares that are at least 2x2 in size, and when all four corner crates match, they slide together and merge into one big crate.

After a successful merge, watch as a forklift rolls in to pick up the big crate and carry it away along its path — adding a rewarding visual touch to every move. The game provides clear visual feedback while you select crates: corner pieces pulse and glow when a valid square is formed, making it easy and intuitive to play.

Need a little help? Use powerful power-ups to remove stubborn crates, shuffle the entire board, or create new match opportunities. As levels progress, the puzzles grow more challenging — you'll need to plan ahead, use your moves wisely, and think strategically to clear the required number of crates before your moves run out.

With smooth animations, particle effects, and progressively challenging levels, Cargo Square Match delivers a gameplay experience that's both relaxing and exciting. Whether you have a few minutes or an hour, every session is rewarding.

Square Drawing

Power-Ups
