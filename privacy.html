01 — Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Cargo Square Match ("the App") handles user information. The App is a puzzle game published on the Apple App Store by Aaqib Javed. We respect your privacy and have designed the App with data minimization in mind.

02 — Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data from users of this App. Cargo Square Match operates entirely on your device without requiring an account, login, or any form of personal input.

All game progress, including levels completed, scores, and power-up usage, is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

03 — Categories of Data Not Collected

For transparency, the following types of data are explicitly not gathered by the App:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • Geographic location or GPS coordinates
  • Device advertising identifiers or fingerprints
  • Photos, camera data, microphone input, or media files
  • Contacts, calendars, or address book entries
  • Payment details or financial records
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic data transmitted externally
  • Health, fitness, or biometric information

04 — Third-Party Integrations

Cargo Square Match does not embed third-party advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, or tracking libraries. The App does not share data with third parties for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose.

App Store Distribution: The App is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may independently collect certain data (such as download statistics) in accordance with their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

05 — Audience & Children's Safety

Cargo Square Match is rated 4+ and designed to be enjoyed by players of all ages. Because no personal data is collected, the App inherently complies with children's privacy regulations including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) provisions for minors.

Parents and guardians can feel confident that their children's information is never at risk while using this App.

06 — On-Device Data & Security

Your puzzle progress, preferences, and power-up inventory are stored exclusively on your device using standard local storage mechanisms provided by iOS. This data is protected by your device's built-in security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, encryption at rest).

Since no data is transmitted to external servers, the risk of network-based data breaches is eliminated. Uninstalling the App will permanently erase all locally stored game data.

07 — Your Choices & Control

You maintain full control over your experience with Cargo Square Match:

  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the App from your device at any time
  • Manage App permissions (such as notifications) through iOS Settings
  • Reach out to us with questions or concerns using the contact information below

Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete on your behalf.

08 — Policy Updates

Should we make changes to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes, if any, will be communicated through the App or our website. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
